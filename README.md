NYC Building Energy Prediction — Hackathon Use Case 2

About the Project

Our team is using data from NYC apartment buildings to predict how much energy they use each year.

We also want to identify buildings that use more energy than expected.

Our Goals

Data Cleaning: Prepare the data for analysis.

EDA: Explore patterns in building energy usage.

Machine Learning: Build models to predict energy consumption.

Model Evaluation: Compare our predictions with actual energy usage.

Presentation: Share our findings and results.

Dataset

We have two datasets:

Training: 9,976 buildings

Testing: 2,495 buildings

Tools

Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, and Scikit-learn.
